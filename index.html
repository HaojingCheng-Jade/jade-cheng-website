About Me 😃

Hi, I'm Jade — a second-year Master's student in Statistical Science at Duke, graduating in May 2027. I'm looking for full-time Data Science or Data Engineering roles where I can own problems end to end: from messy raw data to models, pipelines, and dashboards that help people make real decisions.

When a Netflix Show Sparked a Career Path

My path into data started with an unexpected source: House of Cards. Learning how Netflix used viewing behavior — pauses, searches, ratings — to shape casting and storylines was the moment it clicked for me. Data wasn't just numbers on a screen; it was how companies understood people and made better calls.

That curiosity led me to a Bachelor's in Data Science at Communication University of China. Since then, I've gravitated toward work that sits at the intersection of analytics and engineering — building the pipelines that make analysis possible, then turning the results into something stakeholders can actually use.

What I've Been Working On

Most of my experience falls into two buckets: building data infrastructure, and using that data to answer business questions. A few highlights:

Lenovo — Price Sensitivity Analysis

Jul 2026 – Present | Data Scientist | Morrisville, NC

  • Built an end-to-end ETL pipeline to clean and integrate 4 years of pricing, sales, revenue, and product data for price sensitivity modeling.
  • Developed an ML pricing system to forecast units sold, revenue, and margin under different pricing scenarios.
  • Deployed backend prediction services and interactive dashboards on AWS (S3, Athena) so business users can compare scenarios and understand key drivers.

Cormetech — Manufacturing Data Engineering

May 2026 – Present | Data Engineer Intern | Durham, NC

  • Built automated ETL pipelines with Python, SQL Server, and Airflow to move production data into standardized tables for reporting.
  • Encoded machine operating logic to flag running, downtime, changeover, and abnormal modes — enabling production metrics and anomaly analysis.
  • Developed interactive HTML dashboards per production step; migrated 500+ production order records with redesigned table schemas and relationships.

Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (ICBC)

Jun 2023 – Aug 2023 | Data Analyst Intern | Beijing, China

  • Queried customer activation, transaction, and app-binding data with SQL; used Python to analyze back-to-school demand across card activation and digital channels.
  • Built Power BI dashboards for activation funnels, conversion rates, and channel performance — giving stakeholders a self-serve view of digital adoption trends.
  • Designed A/B tests for email, SMS, and in-app campaigns, contributing to a 5.8% lift in card-binding conversion rate.

Across these roles, my toolkit has grown to include Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn), SQL, Airflow, AWS, statistical modeling, A/B testing, and LLM APIs — but what matters more to me is knowing when to use each one, and making sure the output lands with the people who need it.
